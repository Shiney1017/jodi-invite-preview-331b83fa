A note to the Woods family

Hi Jodi, Jon, and the entire Woods family!

I wanted to take a moment to personally thank you all for the trust, loyalty, and support you've shown Shine My Ride 757 over the past few years.

When I first started this business, I was just a young man fresh out of the Army, trying to build something from the ground up and putting myself out there on Facebook and Nextdoor, hoping someone would give me a chance. You were among the people who believed in that vision before there was much of a business to show for it.

You've been with us through the growing pains, the lessons learned, and the inevitable challenges of entrepreneurship. Through it all, you've continued to trust us with your vehicles and support what we're building. That means more to me than you probably realize.

As we continue to grow, I never want to lose sight of the people who helped us get here. Customers like you aren't just appointments on our calendar. You're part of our story, and your loyalty has played a meaningful role in helping us become what we are today.

With that said, we're incredibly excited to officially welcome the Woods family to The Shine Club! This is more than just a detailing membership. It's an early glimpse into something much bigger we're building through SRVUZ, the next chapter of our vision to bring dependable, premium services and an exceptional customer experience to more people.

And as we take that next step, it means a lot to have the people who believed in us from the beginning growing alongside us.

Thank you all for taking a chance on that local young man from Facebook and Nextdoor all those years ago. I haven't forgotten where we started, and I certainly haven't forgotten the people who helped us get here.

We're grateful for the entire Woods family and look forward to taking care of you for many more years to come!

With sincere appreciation,

Jay & Danielle
